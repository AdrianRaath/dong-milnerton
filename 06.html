Meet the Dongfeng 06

The Dongfeng 06 is built for those who want it all - space, style and electric efficiency in one versatile package. With a bold silhouette and a confident stance, the 06 delivers SUV practicality without compromising on modern design.

Whether you're navigating city streets or heading out on a weekend adventure, the 06 combines generous interior space, a capable electric drivetrain and smart technology into a driving experience that adapts to your lifestyle.
