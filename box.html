Meet the Dongfeng Box

The Dongfeng Box redefines urban mobility with its bold, distinctive design and compact footprint. Perfect for city driving, this all-electric vehicle combines playful aesthetics with practical performance.

With a range that handles your daily commute and weekend adventures, the Box delivers everything you need in an eco-friendly package that stands out from the crowd.

430km Range
43.9kWh Battery
70kW Power

Custom Sport Rims

Lightweight alloy sport rims combine stunning visual appeal with optimized aerodynamics for improved range and performance.

Safety First

Your protection is our priority. The Box is equipped with advanced safety features.

Adaptive Cruise Control

Automatically adjusts your speed to maintain a safe distance from the vehicle ahead, making highway driving effortless and safe.

Intelligent Brake Assist

Detects emergency braking situations and applies maximum braking force to help reduce stopping distance and avoid collisions.

Lane Departure Warning

Monitors lane markings and alerts you when the vehicle begins to drift out of its lane without signaling.

Comfort Without Compromise

Multifunction Steering Wheel

Smart Discreet Storage

One Touch Seat Reclining

Spacious Back Seats
