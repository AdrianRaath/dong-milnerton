Who We Are

Welcome to DongFeng Milnerton, proudly operated by Autohaus Angel, a trusted name in the Western Cape automotive industry for 28 years and counting.

We are proud to be the first official DongFeng dealership in the Western Cape, bringing innovative, high-quality vehicles to the region, backed by decades of dependable service. As part of the Autohaus Angel family, our dealership is built on a strong foundation of integrity, reliability, and customer-focused care.

Why DongFeng

DongFeng is a globally recognised automotive brand known for advanced engineering, durability, and exceptional value. With over 55 years of expertise and partnerships with some of the world's leading automakers, DongFeng brings groundbreaking innovation and visionary mobility to South Africa.

Our experienced team is dedicated to helping you find the right vehicle to suit your lifestyle or business needs, offering professional service with a friendly, personal touch.
